Recover my trades

ESPN only shows what was traded in a past trade to the managers whose team was in it. Share your ESPN login with this site once and it fills in every past trade your team made, for everyone in the league to see.

Your ESPN login cookies are sent once over a secure connection, used only to read this league's past trades, and then thrown away: they're never saved, and nobody else, the commissioner included, can see them. Only share them with this league's own site.

The quick way (on a computer)

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Send ESPN login
    In Edge it's the favorites bar: press Ctrl+Shift+B to show it first.
  2. Go to fantasy.espn.com and sign in.
  3. Click the Send ESPN login bookmark. This site opens in a new tab and fills in your trades.
On a phone

Tap , then bookmark any page, edit that bookmark, and paste over its address. Name it Send ESPN login. While signed in on fantasy.espn.com, open the bookmark: in Chrome, type its name in the address bar and tap it; in Safari, pick it from your bookmarks.

No bookmarks? Use the browser console instead

  2. In the ESPN tab, open the console: press F12 and choose Console (or Ctrl+Shift+J; on a Mac ⌥+⌘+J).

  4. ESPN says "Sent!". Come back to this tab and your trades fill in.

Or copy the cookies yourself
  1. On a computer, sign in at espn.com.
  2. Open the developer tools (F12, or right-click the page and choose Inspect).
  3. Go to Application (Chrome, Edge) or Storage (Firefox), then Cookies, then https://fantasy.espn.com.

Sync with ESPN

Rosters and standings update when syncing the current season. ESPN keeps the activity feed only for the current season, so earlier seasons come from its transaction log instead: every add, drop and waiver claim, but for many trades only the date and who accepted. Those show up as trades for members to fill in with Edit. Draft picks and anything added to a transaction are never overwritten by a sync.
